# Annual Leave Policy — synthetic example
## Leave entitlement
Employees receive 15 days of annual leave per calendar year, effective 1 January 2026. Leave is prorated for employees joining during the year.
## Request procedure
Submit a leave request to your line manager at least three working days before the planned leave. The manager reviews coverage and approves the request before leave begins.
## Carry forward
Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March.
